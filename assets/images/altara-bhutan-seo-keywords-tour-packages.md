# Altara — Bhutan SEO Keyword & Tour Package Research

Prepared as an SEO specialist / content strategy brief. Covers top-performing search keywords for Bhutan travel, plus 30 tour package concepts (6 each across Cultural, Trekking, Festival, Adventure, and Luxury) modeled on the itinerary names and structures that currently rank and convert best across major Bhutan tour operators.

---

## 2. Cultural Tours (6)

1. **7-Days Essential Bhutan** — Paro, Thimphu, Punakha classic circuit
2. **10-Days Discover Bhutan** — extended cultural circuit adding Gangtey/Phobjikha
3. **Bhutan Dzong & Monastery Heritage Tour** — Paro Rinpung Dzong, Punakha Dzong, Tashichho Dzong
4. **Tiger's Nest & Sacred Valleys Tour** — Paro Taktsang-centered short cultural break
5. **Bhutan Cultural Wonder Tour** — 3–4 night "glimpse of Bhutan" entry-level itinerary
6. **Bhutan Heritage & Craft Villages Tour** — textile academy, Craft Bazaar, farmhouse visits, rural heritage focus

---

## 3. Trekking Tours (6)

1. **Druk Path Trek** — 5–6 days, Paro to Thimphu, most accessible / beginner-friendly
2. **Jomolhari Trek (Jomolhari Basecamp Trek)** — 8–11 days, Bhutan's signature high-altitude trek
3. **Snowman Trek** — 20–25 days, widely cited as one of the hardest treks in the world
4. **Laya Gasa Trek** — 14–18 days, extends past Jomolhari into Laya and Gasa communities
5. **Dagala Thousand Lakes Trek** — quieter, lake-focused alternative to Druk Path
6. **Bumdra Trek (Bumdrak Hike)** — short 2–3 day trek ending near Tiger's Nest, strong for time-limited travelers

---

## 4. Festival Tours (6)

1. **Paro Tshechu Festival Tour** — late March/early April, Bhutan's most photographed festival, Rinpung Dzong Thongdrel unveiling
2. **Thimphu Tshechu Festival Tour** — September, largest festival, easiest logistics for first-timers
3. **Punakha Drubchen & Tshechu Tour** — February, unique historical battle re-enactment + classical Tshechu, set at Punakha Dzong
4. **Jambay Lhakhang Drup Tour** — October/November, Bumthang, famous for the fire-blessing ("naked dance") ceremony
5. **Black-Necked Crane Festival Tour** — November, Phobjikha/Gangtey, wildlife + culture crossover
6. **Gangtey / Wangdue Tshechu Tour** — September, Phobjikha valley, less crowded alternative festival circuit

---

## 5. Adventure Tours (6)

1. **Punakha White-Water Rafting Tour** — Mo Chhu & Pho Chhu rivers, Grade II–IV rapids, half/full-day options
2. **Bhutan Mountain Biking Tour** — Dochula Pass and Punakha Valley routes
3. **Trans Bhutan Trail Multi-Activity Tour** — hiking, cycling, rafting combined itinerary
4. **Bhutan Camping & Homestay Adventure Tour** — village homestays paired with light trekking
5. **Bhutan Wildlife & Birdwatching Adventure Tour** — Royal Manas National Park, Phobjikha Valley, 400+ bird species
6. **Hiking, Cycling & Rafting Combo Tour** — Druk Path hike + cycling + Punakha rafting, positioned as the "active traveler" flagship

---

## 6. Luxury Tours (6)

1. **Amankora Journey (Multi-Lodge Circuit)** — Paro, Thimphu, Punakha, Gangtey, Bumthang lodge-to-lodge, the benchmark luxury product
2. **Six Senses Bhutan Wellness Journey** — 5-lodge wellness circuit, strongest positioning for honeymoon/wellness search intent
3. **COMO Uma Paro & Punakha Luxury Tour** — 2-lodge boutique circuit, popular shorter luxury alternative
4. **Bhutan Luxury Honeymoon Package** — Amankora or Six Senses-based, private guide/driver, spa inclusions
5. **Taj Tashi / Zhiwa Ling Heritage Luxury Tour** — single-property luxury stay for shorter 5–7 night trips
6. **Bhutan Private Helicopter & Luxury Lodge Tour** — top-tier itinerary combining lodge stays with optional heli transfers/scenic flights

---

## Notes on how these were selected

These aren't invented itinerary names — each one mirrors naming conventions and structures currently used by established Bhutan tour operators (Druk Asia, Ace the Himalaya, Thrillophilia, Asia Odyssey Travel, Yak Holidays, and others), which is deliberate: matching the naming patterns travelers already search for gives new content a head start on relevance, rather than competing against unfamiliar terminology. Festival dates shift year to year (lunar calendar-based) — treat the months cited above as directional and confirm exact 2027 dates before publishing any date-specific landing page.
