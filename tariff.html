The Sustainable
Development Fee

Bhutan does away with the fixed daily package rate you may have read about — since September 2022, every international visitor instead pays a Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) directly to the government, on top of whatever a private trip with Altara costs. The SDF funds free healthcare and education, environmental conservation, and infrastructure for Bhutanese citizens, so every night you spend in the kingdom is also an investment in it. Figures below reflect the current published rate — we always confirm the exact fee for your dates before you book.

International Visitors

$100Per Person, Per Night

Ages 13 and above. Paid directly to the Bhutanese government as the Sustainable Development Fee, in addition to your trip cost.

Children 6–12

$50Per Child, Per Night

A 50% discount on the standard SDF rate applies automatically for children aged 6 through 12 traveling with family.

Children Under 6

FreeSDF Exempt

No Sustainable Development Fee applies to children under 6 years old at the date of travel.

Regional travelers from India, Bangladesh and the Maldives pay a separate, lower fee structure — ask your planner for the current rate.

Visa & Payment

Most nationalities need a visa to enter Bhutan, arranged for you through Altara as your licensed operator once your trip is confirmed — there's a separate one-time visa processing fee, payable in advance. Travelers from India, Bangladesh and the Maldives do not require a visa.

Since Bhutan has no fixed daily package rate, every itinerary is quoted individually based on season, pace and accommodation. Your planner will confirm the full breakdown — trip cost, SDF and visa fee — in writing before you pay any deposit.
