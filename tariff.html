The Sustainable
Development Fee

Bhutan does away with the fixed daily package rate you may have read about — since September 2022, every international visitor instead pays a Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) directly to the government, on top of whatever a private trip with Altara costs. The SDF funds free healthcare and education, environmental conservation, and infrastructure for Bhutanese citizens, so every night you spend in the kingdom is also an investment in it. Figures below reflect the current published rate — we always confirm the exact fee for your dates before you book.

What's Included

Included

  • Private licensed guide and driver throughout
  • Boutique and luxury accommodation
  • All meals during your itinerary
  • All internal transport and permits

Not Included

  • International flights to and from Bhutan
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal expenses and gratuities
  • Alcoholic beverages

Most nationalities need a visa, arranged for you through Altara once your trip is confirmed — a separate one-time visa fee applies, payable in advance. Travelers from India, Bangladesh and the Maldives don't require one. Since Bhutan has no fixed daily package rate, every itinerary is quoted individually — your planner confirms the full breakdown (trip cost, SDF and visa fee) in writing before any deposit.
